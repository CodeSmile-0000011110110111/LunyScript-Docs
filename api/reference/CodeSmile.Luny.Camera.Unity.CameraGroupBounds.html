The walk bounds of a camera group: the Inspector setting, beside a Cinemachine target group, that says what the group's shot does when its members spread. FrameEveryone sets no limit; KeepInShot keeps the group within Width by Depth and holds the shot of that rectangle; ZoomOutThenKeepInShot keeps the same limit and lets the shot zoom with the members' spread up to it.

Remarks

The camera group decides, LunySteer moves. This component never writes a member's position. It decides the step each member may take in a frame; LunySteer's SteerGroup beside it collects the frame's steps of the members' SteerBody components, asks this component, and moves the bodies. A member that nothing steers still counts towards the group's spread and stands still for it.

The members are the target group's: every target with an object that is active and a weight above zero, each with the target's radius as its footprint. A game that fills the group itself, and LunyCoop's shared view, which adds every local pawn, both decide who is held. Dropping a member from the shot, or moving it back after a timeout, is the game's: take it out of the target group and it walks free.

The fixed shot. With KeepInShot this component adds four corner objects of its own to the target group, at the corners of a Width by Depth rectangle centred on the members, each with the largest member radius, so the group's Cinemachine framing frames that rectangle and does not zoom as the members spread: every member's sphere lies within the corners' spheres from any camera angle while the members stand within the bounds. Cinemachine's perspective group framing fits approximately: on a test camera set up like the co-op arena's, four members walking from the centre to the corners of 20 by 12 metres moved the fixed shot's camera from 28.85 to 29.32 metres from the ground it looks at, where the zooming shot went from 7.78 to 29.52. With either other policy the members alone are framed, and the framing zooms as Cinemachine's own group framing allows: the rig's range has to reach the shot of a Width by Depth group for ZoomOutThenKeepInShot to keep every member in the shot.

Steady state allocates nothing: the corner objects are created once when the policy first needs them.

Methods

RoomFor(float, Vector2, out Vector2, out Vector2)

The part of the ground a new member with this footprint may stand on without widening the group past its bounds: low and high are its corners, with the world's X in x and its Z in y. An axis with no room, because the group has no member or is already wider than its limit there, collapses to point; an axis without a limit is unbounded.
