Interface ICloudClock

Namespace
CodeSmile.Luny.Cloud
Assembly
ApiSource.dll

The two times the client reads: the device's UTC time, which a queued score records as the moment of its Submit (C24 A compares it with a season's end), and a monotonic count of seconds, which spaces the score queue's retries. The domain's seam over System.DateTime.UtcNow and System.Diagnostics.Stopwatch; AGENTS.md section 4.
