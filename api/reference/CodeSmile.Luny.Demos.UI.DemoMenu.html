The installed DemoSupport's Demo menu: a shared flow its opener owns, opened by the canonical Demo action, F1 or Tab on a keyboard and View or Select on a gamepad, and closed by the same press, Menu or Back. It holds the commands the scene installs, such as reset, respawn, the genre profile, an assist, the layout and the scene comparisons; each item runs its command through the host's CodeSmile.Luny.UI.UICommands, so a command the scene did not bind, or one its capability policy refuses for the opener, stays visible and disabled with its reason.

Remarks

The menu uses LunyUI's common flow, focus, time and cancellation rules: a Demo press while another shared flow is open does nothing, and the host's time policy decides whether game time freezes. DemoSupport runs no reset, mount or weapon logic of its own; the commands call their owners.
