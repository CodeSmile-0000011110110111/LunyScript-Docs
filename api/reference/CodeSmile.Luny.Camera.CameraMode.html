The standard camera modes a CameraShot configures a Cinemachine camera for.

public enum CameraMode
Expand Details ...

Fields

FirstPerson = 1

At the target, looking where it looks: a perspective lens of 60 degrees by default.

Isometric = 3

Above the target at a fixed angle, 60 degrees of yaw and of pitch by default, with an orthographic lens of size 10.

ThirdPerson = 2

Behind the target at a distance, 2 units by default, looking where it looks: a perspective lens of 60 degrees by default. Cinemachine's third-person follow keeps the camera out of walls when its template enables that.

TopDown = 4

Straight above the target, 90 degrees of pitch, with an orthographic lens of size 10.

Examples

var shot = CameraShot.Of(CameraMode.TopDown);
