Struct WaitBuilder

Namespace
CodeSmile.LunyScript

What Wait(amount) returns: a routine step that holds its routine for that much game time. Used as a block it waits that many seconds; Seconds(), Milliseconds(), Minutes() and Hours() name the unit.

public readonly struct WaitBuilder
Expand Details ...

Examples

On.Ready(Routine("Door").Run(Opening.Set(true), Wait(3), Opening.Set(false)));

Remarks

Seconds is the default unit, so Wait(3) and Wait(3).Seconds() are the same wait. The wait counts game time from the moment the routine reaches it, and the amount is read then. Zero finishes at once; a negative amount, NaN or an infinity finishes at once and is reported as a warning. docs/proposals/wait-step.md is the contract.
