Class PrefabBinding

Namespace
CodeSmile.Luny.Spawn.Unity
Assembly
ApiSource.dll

One prefab a recipe names by key: the prefab, whether its copies are pooled and how many may exist, the space it needs where it spawns, and a profile text its participants receive, such as a Combat profile. A SpawnBindings asset holds them and the Inspector edits them.
