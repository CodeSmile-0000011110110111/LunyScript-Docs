AsExplosion(Vector3ValueBlock, NumberValueBlock, string, int)

Pushes the body away from a world point the way Unity's Rigidbody.AddExplosionForce does: the strength falls off linearly from the centre to zero at the radius, measured to the closest point of the body's colliders, and a body whose colliders are all farther away than the radius is not pushed. Required; without it the chain is unfinished.

Remarks

The explosion acts on this script's own body only and finds no other body; a script on each body that should be pushed runs its own explosion. The radius must be a number greater than 0 when the block runs; otherwise it is reported once and nothing is pushed.

LunyScriptUsageException

center or radius is null, or this builder is a default that no verb produced.
