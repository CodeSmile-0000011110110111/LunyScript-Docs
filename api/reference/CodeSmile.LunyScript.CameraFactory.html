The Camera surface inside Build(): what one camera rig tracks. It hides UnityEngine.Camera inside a Script the same way Transform hides UnityEngine.Transform.

public readonly struct CameraFactory
Expand Details ...

Examples

On.Ready(Camera.For(ChaseRig).Follow());

Remarks

A rig is an authored Cinemachine configuration and this surface does not replace it. Framing, damping, noise and collision stay in the rig the author set up; what a script writes is which objects the rig tracks. That is what the captain's answer on the camera family requires: the camera is routed through Cinemachine rather than reimplemented over it.

Follow and look-at are two targets, not one. A chase camera keeps its position relative to the car it follows and aims at the corner ahead of it, so a rig carries both and each is written on its own.

This surface's shape is provisional, for the reason ViewFactory states: it implements the recorded recommendation of the open decision contract-view-routing.
