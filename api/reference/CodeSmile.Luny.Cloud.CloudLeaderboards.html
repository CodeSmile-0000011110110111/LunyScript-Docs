Remarks

The score queue. A Submit enters a bounded queue on the device (QueueCapacity scores), written to the client's ICloudQueueStore at each change so a restart keeps it, and is sent for the account it was submitted for, oldest first, while that account is signed in; a score submitted while signed out belongs to the next account that signs in. Before sending, the queue reads the board's seasons; a season that ended after the Submit ends the score as Expired, unsent (C24 A). A season read that fails, and a send refused as Offline or RateLimited, leave the score waiting; the queue tries again after QueueRetrySeconds, or at the next Submit or sign-in. Any other outcome of a send ends the score, and a score is never sent again after the service may have taken it (the 2026-09-09 queue contract).

Reads do not wait for the queue (docs/proposals/lunycloud.md L13): a page read right after a Submit can come back before the score arrived.
