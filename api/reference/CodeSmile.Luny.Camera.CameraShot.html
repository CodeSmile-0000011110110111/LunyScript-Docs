Class CameraShot

A camera mode and its standard values: the part of a Cinemachine camera's setup that LunyCamera writes when it applies the shot. Everything else on the camera, its targets, its output channel, its extensions and the settings of its components that no value here names, stays as the camera's template or the scene authored it. A shot is immutable: each With call returns a changed copy.

Remarks

What each mode writes, on the Cinemachine camera the shot is applied to:

FirstPersonA perspective lens of FieldOfView; a CinemachineFollow locked to the target with no offset and DampingX, DampingY and DampingZ as its position damping; a CinemachineRotateWithFollowTarget.
ThirdPersonA perspective lens of FieldOfView; a CinemachineThirdPersonFollow with Distance as its camera distance and the damping as its damping; no aim component. Its obstacle avoidance stays as authored.
Isometric and TopDownAn orthographic lens of OrthographicSize; the camera's rotation from Pitch and Yaw; a CinemachineFollow in world space that keeps the camera Distance back along that rotation, with the damping as its position damping; no aim component.

A component of another kind in the body or aim stage is replaced by the mode's, so the shot's values take effect. The defaults are the camera family's accepted ones; damping starts at zero.
