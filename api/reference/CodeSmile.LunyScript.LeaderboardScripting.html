Class LeaderboardScripting

Namespace
CodeSmile.LunyScript

The Leaderboard verbs a script writes inside Build(): submit a score to a declared board, and read a page of its entries or one account's entry (C06 A, C07 B, C23 A). Outcomes arrive through When.Leaderboard(handle).

public static class LeaderboardScripting

Examples

On.Message(RunEnded, Leaderboard.Submit(Score).To(Weekly).As(Submitting));
On.Ready(Leaderboard.Load(Weekly).Into(TopTen).As(LoadingBoard));

Remarks

A score waits in LunyCloud's durable queue on the device until it is sent for the account it was submitted for; a season of its board that ended after the Submit ends it as Expired, unsent (C24 A), and board.Waiting counts the scores waiting (C25 A). A read does not wait for waiting scores. docs/proposals/lunycloud.md is the contract.
