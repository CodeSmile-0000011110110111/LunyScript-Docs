Struct ViewId

Namespace
CodeSmile.Luny.Camera
Assembly
ApiSource.dll

The identity of one view: a number LunyCamera gives each view it creates or binds and never gives again in the same play session. A view keeps its identity across scene loads while its output exists; a participant who leaves and a guest who takes the seat afterwards have two views with two identities, so a consumer that keeps an identity never mistakes the second for the first.
