What an action reports about the objective it was given: still pending, finished having met it, or finished having failed to meet it.

public enum ActionResult
Expand Details ...

Fields

Failed = 2

The action finished without meeting its objective.

Running = 0

The objective is still pending; the owner advances this action again next frame.

Worked = 1

The action finished and met its objective.

Examples

public override ActionResult Advance(ScriptInstance instance) =>
    instance.GetNumber(m_RemainingSlot) > 0.0 ? ActionResult.Running : ActionResult.Worked;

Remarks

These are the three outcome words docs/execution-model.md accepts: Worked and Failed are the two natural finished outcomes and Running describes an objective still pending. An action reports Failed only through its own specified domain-failure behaviour; a C# exception thrown out of an action is not a Failed result, it is a contained action failure that stops the instance, and docs/execution-model.md, "Contained action failures", owns that path.

Cancellation is not a member of this enum, deliberately. Cancellation interrupts work and is neither Worked nor Failed, so it is expressed by Cancel(ScriptInstance) rather than by a fourth result. How an interruption is represented to a script is open point G2 in docs/execution-model.md.
