A bounded area: every candidate is drawn uniformly from its disk, box or ball, in the object's local space, and moved into the world through the object's transform as it is at the attempt. The object's rotation is the rotation every arrival takes.

Remarks

A disk and a ball are sampled uniformly by area and by volume: the radius is drawn as the square root and the cube root of a uniform number. A box is sampled uniformly on each axis. Rejection of a blocked candidate is bounded by the attempt budget and the member's deadline.
