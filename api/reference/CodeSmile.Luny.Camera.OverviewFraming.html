Class OverviewFraming

Namespace
CodeSmile.Luny.Camera
Assembly
ApiSource.dll

The orthographic size and the pitch that frame a group from above, solved together in one update (CC72 of the default controller interview, F4 of its review): the pitch follows the size through a coupling of sizes and pitches, interpolated between its points and clamped at its ends, and the size is the smallest that keeps every member's footprint inside the view at that pitch, the view's yaw and the output's aspect, with a margin, never below the lowest size. A strict top-down view keeps one pitch.

Remarks

The size depends on the pitch and the pitch on the size, so the solution is the smallest size whose own pitch frames the group: the root of the residual between the size a pitch needs and the size itself, found by bisection over a bracket that starts at the lowest size, a standard root-finding method that converges to a fixed width for any continuous residual, with no dependence on the previous frame. Nothing here allocates.
